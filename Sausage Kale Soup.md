

Ingredients
* 500g Celery Root
* 1 large fennel bulb, preferably with lots of fronds.
* 1 large bunch kale.
* 1kg Sweet Italian Sausage
* 8c Chicken Broth


Instructions
1. Decase the sausage if necessary and brown ground sausage meat in the pot.
1. Remove sausage from pot, but leave as much of the sausage fat as possible.
1. Add fennel and sweat it.
1. Add the celery root and some of the broth.
1. Simmer until the veggies are all soft.
1. Blend soup completely.
1. Add the rest of the broth.
1. Bring back to a simmer.
1. Chop kale leaves and add to soup.
1. Simmer until kale is at desired doneness.
1. Turn off stove and add chopped fennel fronds.
