
Ingredients
- 1 White Onion
- 3 Carrots
- 2 tbsp Sambhar Masala (Everest Brand)
- 1 tbsp Chicken Bouillon Powder
- 1 tbsp turmeric
- 6c Water
- 2 tbsp red lentils
- 2 tbsp green lentils
- 1 19 oz can cooked chick peas


Instructions
1. Chop the sweat Onion and Carrots in pot with oil.
2. Add sambhar masala and chicken bouillon powder then sweat another 1 or 2 minutes.
3. Add water, red lentils, green lentils, and chick peas (without bean juice).
4. Simmer for 1 hour.
