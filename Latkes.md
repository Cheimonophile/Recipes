



Ingredients
* 1 lb Russet Potatoes
* 1 Sweet Onion
* 1/2 cup Flour
* 2 large eggs



Instructions
1. Peel, chop, and boil **potatoes**.
2. Chop **onions** and sautée until very soft and starting to brown.
3. Mash **potatoes** in a bowl.
4. Add **onions**, **eggs**, and **flour** to the bowl and mix together.
5. Fry like pancakes until done.
