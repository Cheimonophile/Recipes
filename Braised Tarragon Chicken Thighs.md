

Ingredients
- 6-ish Chicken Thighs
- Salt
- 2 Minced Shallots
- Flour
- 2 cups Chicken Broth
- 1 tbsp Dijon Mustard
- 1 tsp Tarragon


Cookware
- 12 in Stainless Steel Skillet with Tight Fitting Lid

Instructions
1. Dry brine the chicken, preferably overnight, and preferably exposed so they can dry off as well.
2. Put a little bit of oil in the skillet and preheat on high until it just starts smoking.
3. Add the chicken thighs skin-side down and leave alone until they are brown and release easily.
4. Set aside the chicken thighs and most of the oil from the skillet.  Leave enough oil in the skillet to cook the shallots.
5. On a lower temperature, sauté the minced shallots in the skillet with the chicken fat until starting to brown.  Add more of the chicken fat if needed.
6. Make a roux in the chicken fat and shallots.  May require adding more chicken fat.
7. Add the chicken broth, mustard, and tarragon.
8. Add the chicken thighs back to the skillet, skin side up.  Put the lid on the skillet and put in the oven for 1-1.5 hours at 300ºF.
9. Take the lid off, turn the oven up to 500ºF, and turn on convection.  Let the chicken brown like this for 10-15 minutes, keeping an eye on it so it doesn't burn.
10. Remove it from the oven, set the chicken aside to cool, and reduce the sauce if it's not thick enough.
11. Once the sauce is at your desired thickness, it's ready to eat.
