
Ingredients
- Olive Oil
- 1kg Sweet Italian Sausage
- 1/2 cup Celery
- 1/2 cup Carrot 
- 1/2 cup Onion
- 700ish ml Arrabbiata Sauce
- 1 cup Whole Milk
- 1 cup White Wine

Cookware
- An oven-safe pot with a tight fitting lid

Steps
1. In a large pot, brown the **Italian Sausage**.
2. Chop **Celery**, **Carrot**, and **Onion**.
3. Add **Celery**, **Carrot**, and **Onion** to the pot with the sausage and sweat.
4. Add **Arrabbiata Sauce** to the pot, put the lid on the pot, and place in the oven at 300f for 3 hours or until the meat is very tender and juicy, checking every half hour to stir and make sure that it doesn't get too dry.
5. Take the pot out of the oven, remove the lid, and put back on the stove.  Add the **Milk** and cook over medium until the sauce starts to dry up.
6. Do the same thing with the **Wine**.
7. Once the sauce is at your preferred consistency, it's done.
