
Ingredients
- 600-700ish ml Tomato Sauce or Meat Sauce
- 450-500g Pasta
	- Pick a shape that works well with your sauce, e.g. rigatoni for meat sauce so meat can get inside the noodles.
- 3 cups Water
- 1tsp Kosher Salt

Cookware
- A wide pot with a tight fitting lid

Instructions
- Mix **Pasta**, **Sauce**, **Water**, and **Salt** in the pot.
- Add the lid and put in the oven at 350 for 40-60 minutes, or until the pasta is desired softness.
- Remove the lid, turn the oven up to 400, and turn on the convection if possible.  Bake until the top is nice and browned.

Optional
- Mix chunks of mozzarella or another cheese in with the pasta before baking it.
- After removing the lid, add shredded cheese to the top of the pasta so that it browns with the pasta.
