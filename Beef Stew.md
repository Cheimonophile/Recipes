

## Ingredients

- **Beef** - 1.5 kg
- **Onions Yellow** - x3
- **Carrots Large** - x3
- **Rutabaga Large** - x1
- **Butter Salted** - 3 tbsp
- **Flour** - 3 tbsp
- **Wine Red** - 750 ml or 3 cups
- **Mushrooms** (Optional)
- **Garlic** - 4 cloves
- **Salt** - 3 tbsp
- **Olive Oil**
- **Water**

## Cookware

- **Rondeau**
- **Rack**

## Instructions

### Mise en Place

- Separate any **Beef** fat from the **Beef**, if a large chunk of **Beef** was purchased with excessive fat.
- Cut the **Beef** into 1 inch chunks.
- Dice the **Onions**.
- Chop the **Mushrooms**.
- Mince the **Garlic**.
- Chop the **Carrots** into 1/2 inch pieces.
- Chop the **Rutabaga** into 1/2 inch pieces.

### Beef Fat (Optional)

1. Cut the fat out of the **Beef**.
2. Cut holes into large chunks of the fat.
3. Put the **Beef** fat on a **Rack** in the **Rondeau**.
4. Bake in the oven at around 250°F until a lot of the fat has rendered.
5. Remove the fat chunks and **Rack** from the **Rondeau**, but leave the rendered liquid fat in.

### Beef Stew

1. Add 3 tbsp of **Salt** to the **Beef** chunks and let dry-brine.
2. Add **Olive Oil** to the **Rondeau**, if you didn't make **Beef** fat in it.
3. Sauté **Onions** and **Mushrooms** until brown.
4. Add minced **Garlic** and sauté 3 mins or until fragrant.
5. Make a roux in the **Rondeau** with the **Butter**, **Flour**, and the veggies.
6. Add the **Wine** bit by bit until the roux is fully incorporated without any lumps.
7. Add the **Beef**, **Carrots**, and enough **Water** to almost cover them.
8. Cook in the oven at 300°F with the lid off, stirring every 30 mins, for about an hour.
	- There should be enough **Water** that it almost but doesn't quite cover the veggies and **Beef**.  They need to stay partially above **Water** level to brown.
9. Add the **Rutabaga**, and add enough **Water** to make sure the vegetables and **Beef** are not quite covered, same as before.
10. Cook in the oven at 350°F, stirring every 30 mins, until the **Beef** is fall-apart tender and the **Rutabaga** and **Carrots** are tender as well.

## Suggestions

- De suggests adding some cinnamon.
- Put some sugar along with the salt when I dry-brine the meat.
- Add some apple cider.
